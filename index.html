Building

Three levels of reality.

PELOCO clearly separates what already exists, what is actively being built and what remains planned.

Exists Today

What exists

The PELOCO token on Solana, public on-chain identity, official X presence, community channels, artwork, documentation and the project manifesto.

Being Built

What is being built

Community, public presence, transparency, documentation, development history, public tracking structure and the credibility that comes from consistent execution.

Planned

What is planned

Creator holdings below 5%, the 4.8–4.9% target, a two-year lock when reached, a separate Public Reserve below 6%, and reinvestment of available resources into the project.

The principle

Intention is not implementation. A goal is not a forecast. An ambition is not a guarantee. Every future element should be clearly identified before it becomes reality.

Long-Term Aspirational Market Cap

Our ambition

Our ambition is to make PELOCO one of the largest and most recognized memecoins in the market. The R$90 billion figure is an aspirational long-term ambition only. It is not a price prediction, valuation expectation or investment promise.

Growing does not mean selling.

PELOCO is not being built with the objective of being sold or having control transferred to a company or third party. The intention is to continue building the project independently.

Risks

Read the risks before the story.

PELOCO is a memecoin. The project cannot remove the risks inherent to the market, blockchain infrastructure or the decisions of participants.

Low liquidity

Low liquidity can cause significant price impact from relatively small transactions and can make buying or selling more difficult.

Volatility

Memecoins can experience extreme price movements in short periods. A token can lose substantial value, including most or all of its market value.

Market & manipulation

Third parties can influence market activity, sentiment, liquidity and price. The project cannot control every participant or market action.

Technical & on-chain

Blockchain, token-program, wallet, infrastructure, platform and smart-contract-related risks may affect availability, trading or access.

Platform & wallet risk

Centralized exchanges, wallets, launchpads, interfaces and other infrastructure are third-party systems and can experience outages, restrictions or failures.

No financial promise

PELOCO does not promise profit, appreciation, financial return, minimum price or success. Every participant must make their own decision.
